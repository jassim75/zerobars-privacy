ZeroBars - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 20 October 2026

ZeroBars is a chat app that works without mobile signal or internet. Messages, photos, videos, voice notes, files and locations go directly from one phone to another over Bluetooth or local Wi-Fi. This policy explains what that means for your information.

No servers, no accounts

ZeroBars has no servers and no user accounts. We (the developer) never receive, store or see your messages, contacts, photos, location or any other personal information. There is no advertising, no analytics and no tracking in the app.

What stays on your phone

Your name and optional profile picture, your chats, and the media you send or receive are stored only on your own phone, inside the app. You can delete a chat, a message or all media at any time in the app. Uninstalling the app deletes everything it stored.

What is shared with nearby phones

  • While ZeroBars is set to Visible, nearby phones running ZeroBars can see your display name, profile picture and a random app ID created on your phone. Choose Hidden to stop this.
  • Anything you send in a chat goes only to the person you send it to.
  • If you turn on Help pass on messages (relay), your phone may carry other people's text messages on to their recipient. These relayed messages are end-to-end encrypted, so your phone cannot read them.

Permissions and why they are needed

  • Nearby devices / Bluetooth: to find other ZeroBars phones and send messages to them.
  • Location: on Android 10 and 11 the system requires it for Bluetooth scanning. On all versions it is also used when you choose Send location, which reads your GPS position once and sends it to that chat only. ZeroBars does not track your location in the background.
  • Microphone: only while you record a voice note. Voice-note transcripts are made on your phone.
  • Notifications and running in the background: so new messages arrive and alert you while the app is closed. A notification shows while ZeroBars is listening.
  • Camera, photos and files: only for items you choose to send.

Children

ZeroBars is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
