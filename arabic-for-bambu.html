Arabic for Bambu - Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 October 2026

Arabic for Bambu turns Arabic text you type into joined, right-to-left letter shapes and saves them as SVG or DXF files for 3D printing and laser engraving.

What the app collects

Nothing. The app works fully offline and has no internet permission. It has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The text you type and your settings stay on your phone.

Files

Files you save go to the Download/Arabic for Bambu folder on your phone. Files you share go only to the app you choose in the Android share menu (for example email or Quick Share). The developer never receives them.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
